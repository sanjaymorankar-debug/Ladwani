## 3. Notable conflict resolutions

- **Posts.** A post in an interest group follows its group's access (their rules); any other post follows our F36 audience. One helper, `src/lib/post-access.ts`, used by comments and reactions.
  - **Moderation:** group posts are moderated by staff or the group's moderators; other posts by our rules (Operators for community-wide posts, the body President / Secretary for District / State / National, Admins for all).
  - **Statuses:** a FLAGGED post (hidden after reports) can be restored with Approve or kept down with Hide. Every rejection still needs a reason.
  - **Post photos:** group posts allow 6 photos visible to group members; feed posts allow 4 visible to the post's audience.
- **Moderation page: a gap of mine, fixed.** In P5 I replaced the post-approval page with the Reported Content page, so waiting posts could only be approved through the API. `/admin/moderation` now shows both:
  - the posts waiting for approval (including flagged group posts), limited to the ones the viewer may decide;
  - the reported-content queue.
- **Bureau, roles, families.** Our versions are kept: the redacted bureau cards, and Admin role changes with the minimum of 2 Admins plus audit and alert. Their case-insensitive search is added to them.
- **`/ladwani` prefix:** about 70 API calls and file links in 35 of our files were missing `BASE_PATH`. All now carry it, and their `scripts/check-base-path.sh` passes.
- **Case-insensitive search:** MySQL compared text without case; PostgreSQL does not. 27 user-typed searches in 8 files now use `mode: 'insensitive'`: audit log, consents, friend search, bureau filters, organisations, tree-link candidates, family filters and the directory.
- **JSON filters in tests:** MySQL's `path: '$.familyId'` became PostgreSQL's `path: ['familyId']`.

## 5. What changes for deploying

- **Migrate.** Back up, then `npx prisma migrate deploy` with `DATABASE_URL_UNPOOLED` (the direct Neon URL). This is `DEPLOYMENT.md` Part 3.
- **Or from SQL:** on an empty database, run `scripts/postgres/01-schema.sql` then `02-reference-data.sql`.
- **At go-live only:** `scripts/postgres/set-death-certificate-required-on.sql`.
- **Data fix (wrong-gender relationships):** run `scripts/postgres/data-fixes/fix_relationship_gender_preview.sql`, then `..._apply.sql`, with `psql` or the Neon SQL editor. Use the `test` branch first. `..._rollback.sql` undoes it.
- **Backups:** `scripts/backup.sh` now uses `pg_dump` with `BACKUP_DATABASE_URL`, the direct URL. Hostinger shared hosting may not have `pg_dump`, so run it from a machine that does, such as a VPS or a CI job.
  - Restore only into a new, empty database: `scripts/restore.sh`, then `scripts/verify-restore.sh`.
  - Neon also keeps point-in-time history. On this project the retention window is short, 6 hours, so the dump is still your own copy.
- **The Neon `test` branch is empty.** Its database `bk_ladwani` has no tables yet, so nothing on it could be previewed. Migrating it is the first step of the test-site setup, and only on your instruction.
